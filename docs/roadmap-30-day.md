# 30-Day Post-Launch Roadmap — SilaiBook

**Vision:** Scale SilaiBook from 1 pilot unit to 50 garment manufacturing units across Surat, Ahmedabad, and Tiruppur hubs.

---

## Phase 1: Days 1–10 (Pilot Stabilisation & Real-World Polish)
- **Goal:** Flawless operation in pilot factory (Shree Ganesh Garments).
- **Key Milestones:**
  1. Monitor daily offline IndexedDB queue sync logs under poor 2G/3G network conditions.
  2. Solicit weekly feedback from tailors on touch number pad responsiveness on low-cost Android phones.
  3. Fine-tune PDF salary slip layout for Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (2-inch / 3-inch roll paper).

---

## Phase 2: Days 11–20 (Multi-Unit Ownership & Advanced Analytics)
- **Goal:** Enable multi-factory owners to manage multiple units from one dashboard.
- **Key Milestones:**
  1. Multi-unit switcher in top navigation for factory owners with 2–5 units.
  2. Batch salary payout helper: Export UPI / Bank NEFT bulk payout files for HDFC & ICICI Corporate Banking.
  3. Operation bottleneck alert: Highlight lots stuck in "Stitching" or "Cutting" for over 48 hours.

---

## Phase 3: Days 21–30 (Commercial Onboarding & WhatsApp Bot Integration)
- **Goal:** Launch self-serve paid subscriptions and automated WhatsApp notifications.
- **Key Milestones:**
  1. Self-serve subscription billing (Razorpay / Cashfree integration at ₹499/month per unit).
  2. Automated daily end-of-day summary message sent to factory owner via WhatsApp Cloud API.
  3. Tailor Self-Verification via SMS/WhatsApp OTP login.
